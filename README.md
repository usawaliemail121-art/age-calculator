# Age Calculator

A free, fast, privacy-friendly age calculator. Enter your date of birth and instantly see your exact age in years, months, and days, plus total days, weeks and hours lived, a next-birthday countdown, and the day of the week you were born.

## Live site

https://usawaliemail121-art.github.io/age-calculator/

## Features

- Exact age in years, months and days (leap-year aware)
- Total days, weeks, hours and months lived
- Next birthday countdown
- Day of the week you were born
- "Age at date" for past/present/future dates
- Fully client-side — no data ever leaves the browser
- SEO-optimised with JSON-LD (WebApplication + FAQPage) structured data
- AdSense-ready (placeholder slots marked in the HTML)

## Tech

Single self-contained `index.html` with inline CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No build step, no external dependencies.
