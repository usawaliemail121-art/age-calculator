About AgeCalculator

AgeCalculator is a free, simple, and private online tool that calculates your exact age from your date of birth. Beyond a basic "years old" figure, it shows your age broken down into years, months and days; the total number of days, weeks, months and hours you have lived; a countdown to your next birthday; and even the day of the week you were born.

Why we built it

Working out an exact age by hand is surprisingly error-prone. Calendar months have different lengths, leap years add extra days, and official forms often ask for age in completed years, months and days as of a specific date. We wanted a tool that gets all of this right every time — and that respects your privacy while doing it.

What makes it different

  • Private by design. Every calculation runs locally in your browser. Your date of birth is never uploaded, logged, or shared.
  • Calendar-accurate. Leap years, 28/29/30/31-day months and "age at date" calculations are all handled correctly.
  • Fast and lightweight. No sign-up, no external libraries, no tracking scripts slowing the page down.
  • Free forever. The tool is and will remain completely free to use.
