How to use the age calculator

Using the calculator takes just a few seconds:

  • Enter your date of birth in the first field. This is the only required field.
  • (Optional) Pick an "age at" date. Leave it blank to use today, or choose any past or future date to see your age on that day.
  • Click "Calculate Age". Your exact age appears instantly — in years, months and days, plus the totals and your next-birthday countdown.
  • Click "Reset" to clear the fields and start over.

All calculations run in your browser. Your date of birth is never uploaded or stored.

Why use an age calculator?

An age calculator comes in handy far more often than people expect:

  • Fill forms correctly. Many official forms — visa, insurance, school admission, medical — ask for age in completed years, months and days on a specific date. This tool gives you that exact figure instantly.
  • Plan milestones. Countdowns to your next birthday, or working out how many days you've been alive, are popular for celebrations and personal milestones.
  • Avoid off-by-one errors. Multiplying your age in years by 365 ignores leap years. The calculator counts real calendar days, so the total-days figure is accurate.
  • Curiosity. Discover the day of the week you were born, or how many weeks you've lived — fun facts that are surprisingly hard to work out by hand.

Frequently asked questions

How is age calculated?
Age is calculated by measuring the time elapsed between your date of birth and the current date (or another chosen date). A complete year is added only once the same month and day as your birth has passed in the target year. For example, someone born on 15 March 2000 turns exactly 25 years old on 15 March 2025 — not on 1 January. Our calculator also breaks the remaining difference into complete months and remaining days, accounting for the different lengths of each month and leap years.
What is chronological age?
Chronological age is the amount of time that has passed since a person was born, measured in years, months, and days from their date of birth. It is the most common and standard way of expressing age, used for legal, medical, educational and administrative purposes. This is in contrast to biological or perceived age, which depend on health, lifestyle and appearance rather than calendar time.
How do I calculate age from date of birth?
Enter your birth date in the field above and click "Calculate Age". The tool computes the difference between today (or your chosen "age at" date) and your birth date, giving you complete years, complete months, and remaining days. You can also see the total number of days, weeks and hours you have lived, plus a countdown to your next birthday.
Does this age calculator account for leap years?
Yes. The calculator works with real calendar dates, so leap years and the different number of days in each month are handled automatically. February is treated as having 29 days in leap years and 28 days otherwise, and the day counts in the totals reflect the actual calendar.
Why does my age in total days not equal years times 365?
Because calendar years are not all the same length. A typical year is 365 days, but a leap year is 366 days, and months vary from 28 to 31 days. Multiplying your age in years by 365 ignores leap years and so produces a slightly smaller number than the true count of days lived. Our total-days figure counts the actual days between your birth date and the target date, which is the accurate figure.
Is my date of birth stored or shared anywhere?
No. All calculations happen entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your date of birth is never sent to a server, stored, or shared with anyone. You can safely use this tool even offline once the page has loaded.
Can I calculate my age at a future or past date?
Yes. Use the optional "Age at date" field to pick any date — past, present, or future. The calculator will tell you how old you were, are, or will be on that exact date, including years, months, days and all the other details.
